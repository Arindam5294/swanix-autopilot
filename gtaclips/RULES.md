# RULES (every GTA VI Clips script reads this; the gate appends its verdicts at the bottom)

## The gauntlet rule
A fresh reviewer that has not seen the script or notes judges every Short, episode and thumbnail.
Nothing posts until it says yes. Copy faults get one automatic fix and a re-check. Visual faults mean no post.

## Content
- Real GTA 6 footage only. No GTA 5, fakes, concepts, mobile clones, leaks, "play early" or free-download scams.
- No spoilers: skip cutscenes, endings, late-story reveals. No exploit or glitch tutorials (they get claimed and struck).
- Credit every creator/streamer with a link in the description. Never imply we are Rockstar or official.
- Remove any clip on request from its creator, same day.

## Clipping campaigns
- Only clip a campaign's streamer from the footage the brief allows.
- Always include the brief's paid label/tags (e.g. #ad). Post from one account. Never edit or delete after submitting.
- Submit the link within 30 minutes of posting (Telegram pings you).
