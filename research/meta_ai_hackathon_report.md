## 🏁 TRACKS / CATEGORIES

### 1. Indie Developer Track
- **For:** Individual builders and students
- **Team size:** Up to 4 members
- **Judging criteria:** Creativity, technical ambition, whether it works
- **Total prize pool:** $400,000

### 2. Startup Track
- **For:** Teams and founders building a real product
- **Team size:** Not explicitly limited (startup team)
- **Representative:** Must be active employee of startup, authorized to receive notices/prize
- **Total prize pool:** $550,000

### Project Themes (apply to both tracks)
1. **Build for Your World** - Bring Meta's models into your domain, solve a problem you know firsthand
2. **Push the Frontier** - Tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, etc.

## 📋 SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

### Format & Requirements
- **Submission portal:** https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit
- **One project per participant/team** - must choose ONE track only
- **Team Representative:** Each team appoints one Representative to submit on behalf of team
- **Representative must:** Identify all team members in submission (Indie track) or be authorized startup employee (Startup track)
- **Demo required:** Working, impressive demo (25% of judging score)
- **Demo video:** Quality of demo video matters

### Judging Criteria (Hackathon Projects - NOT applications)
| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|

| Technical Depth & Execution | 20% | Sound engineering, thoughtful refinement, real craft |
| Demo | 25% | Working impressive demo, holds up live, demo video quality |

### Application Review Criteria (for selection)
- Project idea clarity/feasibility/fit with themes (50%)
- Technical skill shown in described AI project (50%)
- Evaluated on rolling basis, up to 20,000 acceptances
